# Content selection and coverage

**Status:** Phase 1 reference scaffold; operational instructions come in Phase 2.

## Purpose and scope

Own retain/compress/omit decisions and the source-to-guide coverage audit. Include
visual and mathematical source content, not just extracted prose. The v1
specification's C, D, and S govern implementation.

## Key requirements

- Keep important/examinable concepts, formulas, diagram relationships, assumptions,
  and examples with distinct learning value. Slide length never determines importance.
- Compress repetition and low-value logistics, administration, biography, references,
  redundant examples, and filler when appropriate.
- Map each meaningful source item to a guide location or an explicit disposition;
  the audit may remain internal. Resolve important unmapped items before delivery.
- Distinguish course content, explanatory expansion, and optional enrichment where
  expectations depend on it. Unreadable input is an evidence gap, not covered content.
