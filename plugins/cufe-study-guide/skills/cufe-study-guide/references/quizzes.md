# End-of-lecture quizzes

**Status:** Phase 1 reference scaffold; operational instructions come in Phase 2.

## Purpose and scope

Own final understanding assessment and answer separation from requirement P.
Section checkpoints belong to [teaching-style.md](teaching-style.md).

## Key requirements

- Every substantial guide ends with a compact but meaningful quiz unless waived.
- Test understanding of important lecture concepts rather than rote recognition.
- Interpret natural-language preferences for conceptual, scenario-heavy,
  terminology, exam-style, code-tracing, calculation-heavy, or mixed questions.
- Put solutions after the quiz in a separate Answer Key, with appropriate output
  separation so answers are not immediately visible while attempting questions.
- Explain answer reasoning and check correctness against source/guide content.
