# Cross-lecture connections

**Status:** Phase 1 reference scaffold; operational instructions come in Phase 2.

## Purpose and scope

Own relevant prior-material inspection and evidence-based relationships from
requirement O. Accept previous lectures, guides, and supplied folders/paths.

## Key requirements

- Inspect material relevant to the current concepts; do not imply all folder
  contents were reviewed when only some were needed/read.
- Classify useful connections as prerequisite, continuation, contrast, or
  reuse/application. Ground topic and lecture identifiers in actual evidence.
- Include enough recap or a helpful pointer to improve understanding; avoid
  gratuitous connections and invented prior material.
- Resolve meaningful disagreement against available source evidence and disclose
  unavailable material when that limits the requested connection work.
