# Conceptual breakpoints

**Status:** Phase 1 reference scaffold; operational instructions come in Phase 2.

## Purpose and scope

Own natural stopping points for focused study from requirement N. Work at concept
boundaries rather than arbitrary page counts or slide intervals.

## Key requirements

- Aim roughly at 30-minute focused sessions, allowing time for reasoning/practice.
- Prefer conceptual continuity over precise timing; learner pace is variable.
- Keep proofs, dependency chains, worked examples, and tightly coupled explanations
  intact. Do not break before an essential completion of the current concept.
- Mark a boundary only when the learner can safely pause and resume. Make the
  note useful by identifying the completed unit and independence of what follows.
