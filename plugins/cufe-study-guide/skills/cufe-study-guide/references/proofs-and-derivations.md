# Proofs and derivations

**Status:** Phase 1 reference scaffold; operational instructions come in Phase 2.

## Purpose and scope

Own proof inclusion, classification, and source-scope labels from Q and S,
especially for electronics, signals, mathematics, neural networks, and algorithms.

## Key requirements

- Lecture Proof / Derivation: course material from the lecture; preserve important
  reasoning, assumptions, and steps.
- Optional Insight Proof: short added reasoning that deepens understanding; label
  “Understanding aid — not required for memorization.”
- Extended Proof: optional reasoning too long to include; provide a clickable,
  verified link to a clear credible explanation. Do not use this to omit a required
  lecture proof solely for length.
- Keep enrichment separate from course requirements. Preserve logical/mathematical
  accuracy and continuity across study break decisions.
