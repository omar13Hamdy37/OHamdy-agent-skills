# Proofs and derivations

Use for source proofs/derivations, requested mathematical depth, or a candidate
insight that could replace memorization with understanding.

## Select and classify before expanding

1. Is the proof/derivation in the lecture or explicitly required by it? Treat it as
   **Lecture Proof / Derivation** and account for its important reasoning in coverage.
2. Otherwise, does a short argument materially explain the current lecture's idea,
   fit the learner's prerequisites, and respect the user's enrichment preference?
   If yes, include an **Optional Insight Proof**.
3. If useful optional reasoning is too long, technically distant, or distracting,
   offer an **Extended Proof** link when external material is allowed and verifiable.
4. If no category contributes a real learning benefit, omit the added proof.

Not every theorem needs an extra proof. If the user asks for proof/derivation depth,
give appropriate reasoning within scope; that preference does not make every
outside proof a course requirement. Never replace a long required lecture proof
with an optional external link merely to reduce page count.

## Lecture Proof / Derivation

Preserve course notation where possible. State the claim/target, assumptions and
domain, starting facts, decisive steps, and what the result explains. Unpack missing
intermediate algebra or reasoning when it enables the learner to follow the method.
Explain why a step is valid, not only what the next expression is.

Distinguish source steps from explanatory completion if that affects expectations.
If the lecture explicitly assigns a proof but does not provide it, label the
derivation as an explanation of that course requirement, not a verbatim source proof.
If a premise, sign, or step seems wrong, inspect notation/conditions and give a
supported correction or narrow uncertainty. Do not turn a numerical example into
an alleged general proof.

Close with interpretation or applicability. Keep required steps and their dependent
example together under [breakpoints.md](breakpoints.md). Coverage records the target
and reasoning, not merely the theorem name.

## Optional Insight Proof

Use when a reasonably short argument resolves a specific “why,” reveals an
assumption, or helps derive rather than memorize an important relationship. If it
requires many new definitions or overwhelms the course explanation, shorten it to
an intuition or consider the extended option.

Label **Optional Insight Proof** and **Understanding aid — not required for
memorization.** State the focused benefit, prove only what is needed, and return to
course material. Avoid stacking optional proofs after every formula. Optional
status concerns course scope; explicit learner requests can still make it a
required deliverable for this guide.

## Extended Proof and external evidence

When browsing is available and permitted, locate a clear explanation from
university material, a legally accessible official textbook/resource, a reputable
educational site, or a primary technical reference. Open it, inspect the actual
argument, and check that assumptions/notation and difficulty fit this lecture.
Prefer a direct stable page/section and descriptive clickable link over a search
result or an unexamined citation. Reject low-quality SEO pages and unsuitable
proofs, even if their titles match.

Label the link as optional extended material, summarize what it explains, and
mention any important prerequisite. A reachable page alone does not prove relevance.
If no trustworthy match is found, say so rather than recommending a weak source.

With no browsing or unverifiable access, do not fabricate a URL; say an external
proof link could not be verified and continue with supplied material. If the user
prohibits external material, omit external retrieval and links altogether. A
verification limitation must not be used to silently drop lecture-required reasoning.

Check every included argument for valid assumptions, notation, logic, algebra,
dimensions, and boundary conditions relevant to the discipline. Follow the common
provenance policy in [content-selection.md](content-selection.md), then use
[quality-checklist.md](quality-checklist.md) for final review.
